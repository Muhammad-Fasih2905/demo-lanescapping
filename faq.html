Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about quotes, timing, pricing and how we work.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. We visit your property, take measurements, talk through your goals and give you a free written quote with no obligation.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Chattanooga, TN 37404 and serve Hamilton County, including Red Bank, North Shore, Signal Mountain, Lookout Mountain, East Ridge, Hixson, Ooltewah and Brainerd.

How long does a typical project take?

A flower bed refresh can take a day. Patios and walkways usually take one to three weeks, and larger retaining wall projects can take longer. Your quote includes a timeline.

How much does a patio or retaining wall cost?

Price depends on size, materials, slope and access. After a site visit you will get an itemized quote so you can see exactly where the money goes.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We carry insurance for our crew and work, and we can share proof of coverage on request.

Do you handle drainage problems?

Yes. We install French drains, swales and downspout routing, and we grade yards so water moves away from your home.

What plants work best in Chattanooga?

Native shrubs and perennials that tolerate red clay and humid summers do best. We choose plants for your sun, shade and soil, and explain how to care for them.

Can I see examples of your work?

Yes. Visit our Landscaping page to compare before-and-after transformations, or ask us for references.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance?

Yes. We offer one-time cleanups and seasonal plans covering pruning, edging, mulch and leaf removal.
